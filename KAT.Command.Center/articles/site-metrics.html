Site Metrics

The card is intended to summarize site usage, page traffic, CalcEngine request volume, response time, and errors with trend sparklines.

Its tile and card are titled after the site you are on - Camelot ESS Nexgen Metrics - because these are the only numbers on the dashboard that describe the running site rather than its pipelines.

Work In Progress

Do not rely on current values.Site Metrics currently uses fixed mock values. Refresh, cancellation, busy state, and section behavior are real; the displayed numbers do not change.
