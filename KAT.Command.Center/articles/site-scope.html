Site Scope

Every monitored company on one screen: the last six synthetic runs, how slow they were against expectation, how many warnings and failures have piled up over the last hour, four hours, day and two days, and who is on call when one of them is red.

What it shows

Site Scope reads the Nirvana Performance page - the same page you would otherwise open in a browser and scan by eye. Nothing here is a second opinion: the numbers, the colours and the on-call assignment are all that page's, reformatted so the sites that need attention come first instead of appearing in whatever order the report happens to list them.

It is a scrape, not an API.

That page has no service behind it, so Command Center signs in as you and reads the rendered HTML. It works, and it is checked against a real capture of the page - but a redesign upstream can break it without warning. If Site Scope ever reports something the page itself does not, believe the page.

The KPI tile

One cell per site, worst first, so the question "is anything wrong right now" is answered without reading a single number. Underneath it, the counts, and the two lists that matter in the last hour: what is slowest, and what is erroring.

Site names in the two lists are links: clicking one opens the card below with the grid already filtered to that site, and the scope reset to All so the site being pointed at cannot be filtered back out.

History

Six cells, newest on the left, one per monitored run. The colour is the run's own, taken from the page:

Healthy
The run completed inside expectation.
Slow
The run completed but was slow.
Failed
The run failed.

Hover a cell for its duration, the number of pages it walked and its state. The duration alone is not the story: a failure often returns faster than a healthy run because it stopped early, which is why the colour and not the number decides the row's state.

Average

The site's average round trip, hovering to show the expected round trip alongside it. Over expectation is what the page counts as slow.

1 / 4 / 24 / 48 hours

Each window carries two groups: slowness on the left (warnings, then alerts) and failures on the right. They are cumulative counts over that window, so 48 hours is always at least 24, which is always at least 4. Hover anywhere in a window's column for a three-line tooltip - the window, then slowness, then failures.

Every count reserves room for three digits whether it needs them or not, so the icons line up in one vertical run down the table and the ones digits line up in another. A count past 999 is shown as 999 with a red outline around it - hover the cell to read the real totals.

Filtering

Two controls sit above the grid, at its right edge. The box narrows to sites whose name contains what you type. The drop-down beside it narrows by kind:

  • Sites With Issues - only sites with something in the 1 Hour column: a slow warning, a slow alert or a failure. The last hour, because that is the window that answers "is this happening now".
  • PROD Sites / QA Sites - QA sites are the ones whose name starts with QA_.

The two combine, and the header badge recounts itself against whatever is left, so it never disagrees with the rows underneath it. Like your sort, your filter survives a refresh.

Sorting

Click any header to sort by it; click it again to reverse. Each column starts in the direction you would actually want it - state and history start worst-first, counts start highest-first, site starts A-Z.

Your sort survives a refresh. Site Scope repolls on its own every few minutes, and a poll that silently threw away how you had arranged the grid would be worse than not polling at all.

The title bar repeats the site's dot and spells the state out: OK, Warning or Failure. Below it:

  • On-call - who to reach, on two lines. The address copies when you click it; the envelope beside it opens your mail client. The second line is their phone and the hours they cover.
  • Storyboard - opens the site's runbook in your browser.
  • Maintenance window and Queue - the window the site is expected to be down in, and the queue that owns it.
  • Response time - the expected round trip and the measured average together. The average is coloured by the same rule the grid's Average column uses, so a site cannot read amber in the table and plain here.
  • Last states - the last success and the last failure, each behind the square its colour would have in the History strip.

When it refreshes

Site Scope is the only section that refreshes on a schedule of its own. It updates:

  • with every dashboard refresh, alongside every other section;
  • on its own five-minute timer, matching the source page's own cadence;
  • whenever you press ↻ on the tile or the card.

Site Scope is not scoped to the selected site. The source page reports every company at once, so switching sites in the app bar changes nothing here.
