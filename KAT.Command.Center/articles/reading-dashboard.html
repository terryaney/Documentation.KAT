What's Happening

The list is built from the same facts the rail badges count, so a badge and its rows cannot disagree. There are two exceptions. The Pipeline badge counts approvals you can make, but approvals do not get rows of their own; they appear as chips on the release that has just deployed up to them. And a query set to Exclude from What's Happening (Settings > Work Items) still badges, but lists nothing.

Some rows describe a state: they stay while it is true and go when it is not. Others are news: they have a × and end on their own.

Approval chips show one chip per stage of a release you can approve now, all on that release's newest row: the stage that most recently started or finished deploying. As stages start and finish, the chips move to the newest row, and an older finished row then removes itself. Clicking a chip asks you to confirm, then approves.

Rows about another site select that site when clicked, then open the section. An approval chip approves without changing site; the confirm names the site. More than five rows of one kind fold into a single row that opens their section, and its tooltip lists the first ten. Rows for builds and releases the app is watching are never folded.

Colour in the list

  • The left edge is the row's tone:
    • red something is broken: a failed stage or build, a failing site, an unavailable section, or critical or priority 1 items over their threshold.
    • amber something waits on you: a checkout, or high severity, priority 2 or old items. A checkout of yours turns red once it is older than My checkouts turn red after (Settings > Releases); a team member's stays amber.
    • blue it is in flight.
    • green it has just deployed.
    • No edge means it is for your information only.
  • The icon is the rail icon of the section the row belongs to, in that section's colour.
  • Environment chips are coloured exactly as they are on the Pipeline page: the environment's own colour once deployed, while deploying (with a spinner) or waiting for approval, red when failed, and grey while queued.
  • Red text on a row means an action you took was refused. Hover the row to read TFS's message.

Rail badges

A badge counts what in its section needs you and will still be true after a refresh. A badge is never grey: it takes the worst tone among the things it counts, using the colours above. Work Items and CalcEngines are the exceptions: they split their count into a red badge and an amber badge. A rail group's badges (shown while the group is closed) add up its entries' red and amber counts separately.

CalcEngines Each CalcEngine checkout listed in What's Happening: a red badge for yours held longer than My checkouts turn red after, and an amber badge for the rest of yours plus a team member's once held longer than Show team member checkouts after.   • ! means the section failed to load or is not signed in. Hover it to see why.
  • A group (Work Items, Releases, Sentinel) shows its children's red and amber totals separately while those children are hidden: collapsed or in icons mode.
  • Badges for the selected site (Pipeline and CalcEngines) clear the moment you change site, and come back when the new site loads.
  • In icons mode a badge becomes a coloured dot, so Work Items and CalcEngines can show both a red and an amber dot.
  • Three animated dots on an entry are not a badge. They mean it is being checked: a query refreshing in the background, or Pipeline watching a build or release in flight, or a change you just made.

Diagnostics

Diagnostics, under Tools in the rail, opens a menu beside the rail rather than a page. The app writes what it does - each refresh, every TFS call and how long it took, and any failure - to a daily log file in %LOCALAPPDATA%\KAT.Command.Center\logs, and keeps the last 14 days.

Menu item What it does
Open today's log Opens today's log file in your editor. Before anything has been logged today, it opens the log folder instead.
Snapshot current UI Saves the screen exactly as it is, as an HTML file in the log folder, and names the file in a message at the bottom of the window. Snapshots older than 14 days are deleted.
Open log folder Opens the log folder in File Explorer.

Reporting a problem? Take a Snapshot current UI while the problem is on screen, then use Open log folder and attach the snapshot and today's log (kcc-yyyyMMdd.json) when you contact the KAT team.

Sites with a release in flight

The site picker answers one question about every site at once, without you having to select them one at a time: has its newest release finished publishing to every environment? If it has not, the site carries an amber dot - on its row in the dropdown, and on the closed picker when it is the site you are looking at.

Typing * filters the list to sites carrying a dot. Anything you type after it still has to match as usual, so *sample means outstanding and matches "sample" - not either one on its own. The * only does this at the front of what you type; it is a mode switch, not a wildcard.

The dots are filled in by a background sweep that runs shortly after the app starts and continues to periodically poll in the background. It never blocks anything you are doing: sites light up as their answers arrive, and refreshing a site updates its own dot immediately from data that refresh already fetched. A site the sweep cannot reach keeps whatever dot it had rather than going dark, because a failed call is not evidence that a release finished.
